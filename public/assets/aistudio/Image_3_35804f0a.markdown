## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a focused, high-precision dark atmosphere tailored for quantified self-tracking, metabolic health, and athletic optimization. It targets data-driven individuals, athletes, and performance-minded users who prioritize low-friction consumption of nutritional density and metabolic velocity.

The aesthetic fuses **Precision Minimalism** with **Hyper-Contrast Cybernetic Accents**. Surfaces mimic engineered hardware consoles: deeply recessed, noise-free, and calibrated for ultra-low optical fatigue on OLED panels. Visual energy is strictly directed via precise, saturated neon vectors against pitch-black strata, evoking technical instrumentation rather than a passive lifestyle log.

## Colors

The color palette centers on functional chromatic signaling, where color operates as quantitative data rather than decoration:

- **Surface Neutral Foundations:** Deep obsidian base `#0B0F14` for absolute background ground, elevated to `#121820` for secondary canvas and interactive card shells, and `#1A232E` for hovered or tertiary interactive tiers. Subtle separation is maintained via `#263545` at low opacities.
- **Caloric & Kinetic Energy (Primary):** `#10F984` (Neon Emerald) signals total energy budget, active expenditure, and net caloric equilibrium.
- **Protein Synthesis (Secondary):** `#00E5FF` (Neon Cyan) anchors lean mass development, amino acid goals, and target recovery markers.
- **Lipids & Fats (Tertiary):** `#FF2E93` (Neon Fuchsia) signifies essential fatty acids, hormone regulation thresholds, and dense lipid reservoirs.
- **Carbohydrates & Glycogen (Accent Quaternary):** `#FFD000` (Solar Amber) visualizes rapid-burn substrate availability, glycemic replenishment, and workout fueling.
- **Text & Hierarchy:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` strictly reserved for real-time numeric data values and critical headlines; subdued cool grey `#8E9CAE` for metrics labels and descriptive strings; deep slate `#4A5868` for non-interactive track rails and inactive structural elements.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a high-contrast pairing between **Outfit** and **JetBrains Mono**:

- **Outfit** handles narrative headers, macro-level values, and body copy. Its clean geometric curvature provides instant readability at immense scales without clinical sterile dryness.
- **JetBrains Mono** delivers uncompromising technical fidelity for metadata tags, nutrient ratios, gram markers, unit scales, timestamps, and kinetic percentage differentials.
- **Tabular Figures:** All numeric displays must render using tabular figure alignment (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during live telemetry updates and animated metric adjustments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural rhythm, optimized for edge-to-edge compact data density on mobile and segmented dashboard telemetry on wider screens:

- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column fluid stream with strict `1rem` outer screen gutters. Nutrient breakdowns arrange horizontally in dynamic micro-columns or pinned modular tiles.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters. Metric rings and log streams coexist side-by-side.
